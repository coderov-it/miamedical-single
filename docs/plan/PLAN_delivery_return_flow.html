Order lifecycle: delivery, return and inspection

Every physical step is handled by an agent in the admin panel. The blue steps are new.

Proposed: rental and sale

New step Existing status Time, not a status Needs follow-up Exit

Today

rental:  pending ──pay──▶ paid ──"Confirm return"──▶ fulfilled   (stock +1 straight away)
sale:    pending ──pay──▶ paid ──▶ fulfilled (delivered)
         delivery, missed delivery and pickup happen outside the system

Worked example: 2 beds, booked 10 days from 10 Nov, starting with 3 in stock

 1. Order placed                              → stock 1
 2. Paid                                      → stock 1
 3. 10 Nov  Driver: out for delivery
 4. 10 Nov  Customer not home                 → delivery missed (note)
 5. 11 Nov  Driver: out for delivery           (retry)
 6. 11 Nov  Driver: delivered                 → rental starts 11 Nov, due back 21 Nov
                                                (booked 10 Nov was only the plan)
 7. 21 Nov  Driver: out for pickup
 8. 21 Nov  Driver: product received          → stock 1
 9. Inspector: bed #1 → Stock back             → stock 2
10. Inspector: bed #2 → Needs attention        → stock 2, queue: 1, order → fulfilled
11a. Later: bed #2 Repaired                    → stock 3
11b. Fallback: bed #2 Written off              → stock 2, history notes why

Decisions so far

1. Every physical step (delivery, missed delivery, pickup, return) is an order status an agent sets in the admin panel.
2. "Product received" does not restock. An inspector decides each unit: Stock back, or Needs attention.
3. A rental starts when it is marked delivered. The booked start date is only the plan:
     booked 10 Nov, 10 days, delivered 11 Nov  → due back 21 Nov
     booked 10 Nov, 10 days, delivered  9 Nov  → due back 19 Nov

Open: permission split (order:deliver, rental:inspect, product:stock); returns of sold items.
